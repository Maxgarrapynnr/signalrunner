How to use fundamentals

What the score means

7–10 — High dividend yield, cheap valuation, consistent payer. Worth deep research.

4–7 — Average. May have one strong factor offset by a weak one.

0–4 — Expensive, low yield, or inconsistent. Low priority.

The score ranks stocks relative to each other — not an absolute buy/sell signal.

Workflow

1. Find stocks with score ≥ 7

2. Read their AMMC annual report (click the filing link)

3. If you like the business, set a price target alert in SignalRunner

4. Wait for the alert, check RSI confirmation, decide

Fundamentals decide WHAT. Signals decide WHEN.
